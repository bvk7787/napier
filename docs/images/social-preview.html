M8 Operation Manual, v6.5.2 (2026-04-21), p. 11
Song View Shortcuts
Navigating
• [DIRECTION] Move cursor.
• [OPTION]+[UP or DOWN] Move/scroll the cursor 16 rows up or down.
Playing
• [PLAY] Plays/stops all tracks.
• [SHIFT]+[PLAY] In live mode - Stop a cued or playing track.
• [LEFT]+[PLAY] Cue the selected song row for playback.
• [OPTION]+[SHIFT] Mute current track (release option first to hold
  the mute).
• [OPTION]+[PLAY] Solo current track (release option first to hold
  the solo).
