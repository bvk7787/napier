# How the owner uses the modular rack

Written from racks/usage.yaml (hand-maintained with the owner). Each module's job in the rig, what it pairs with, what the owner likes it near, and whether it could ever leave. Lines under 'Not settled yet' are open questions, not facts.

## How to treat this

Everything here is a working default, not an axiom. Challenge any rule, case mode or module job when there's a clear hypothesis for an alternative: name the default, what changes, and what should play better as a result. The owner decides; an accepted challenge gets written back here.

## Working defaults (challengeable)

- Signal flows left to right in each row, sources on the left and processing toward the right.
- The drums sit in the bottom row, nearest the hands, because they're played live. (modules: Steady State Fate Entity Ultra Kick, Steady State Fate Entity Ultra Perc, Erica Synths Sample Drum)
- The 1U row holds hands-on controls and the output.

## The case

### Main case (example figures, not a real supply)

Mode: one bass voice, a small drum kit and a mixer with one effect.
Supply limits: +12V 2000mA, -12V 1000mA, +5V 500mA.
- Row 1: Steppy for hand-played gates, a buffered mult, Pointeuse, the line out
- Row 2: Atlantix with Maths and Quadrax for modulation, Pam's as master clock
- Row 3: kick, perc, Sample Drum and Ikarie into the Xer Mixa; Beads on a send

## Never firing

- ALM Busy Circuits Pamela's Pro Workout: master clock and clock divisions. Pairs with ALM Busy Circuits PPEXP1. Keep near Steady State Fate Entity Ultra Kick. Clock runs to every drum voice.
- Intellijel Atlantix: bass and acid voice, self-contained. Pairs with Ritual Electronics Pointeuse.
- Intellijel Stereo Line Out 1U: output to the audio interface.
- Noise Engineering Xer Mixa: main mixer, two sends. Keep near Intellijel Stereo Line Out 1U, Mutable Instruments Beads. The line out sits in the 1U row above; the owner accepts the cable run.

## Keeping

- ALM Busy Circuits PPEXP1: Pam's expander. Keep near ALM Busy Circuits Pamela's Pro Workout.
- Erica Synths Sample Drum: breakbeat slicing. Pairs with Intellijel Steppy 1U.
  - Not settled yet: Would a slider per step (Step 8) make slice selection more playable than CV from Quadrax?
- Intellijel Amps: two VCAs for the Atlantix and a drone.
- Intellijel Buff Mult 1U: fans out pitch and clock.
- Intellijel Quadrax: four envelopes or LFOs for drums and filter movement.
- Intellijel Steppy 1U: hand-played gates for the Sample Drum.
- Make Noise Maths: envelopes and slew for the Atlantix. Pairs with Intellijel Atlantix.
- Ritual Electronics Pointeuse: switches between two Atlantix patterns.
- Steady State Fate Entity Ultra Kick: kick.
- Steady State Fate Entity Ultra Perc: snares, hats and toms.

## Could go

- Bastl Instruments Ikarie: stereo filter on the Sample Drum. Pairs with Erica Synths Sample Drum.
- Mutable Instruments Beads: granular texture on a send.

## Considered (not owned)

- Joranalogue Audio Design Step 8: slider per step for Sample Drum slices, advanced by Steppy. Pairs with Intellijel Steppy 1U, Erica Synths Sample Drum.
